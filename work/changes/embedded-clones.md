## Summary

Embedded structural matching has its own lexical and source-region contract.
Coarse seeds identify candidates; ordered structure and declaration
correspondence establish witnesses. The complete contract requires original
resource grants throughout collection, proof, reporting and adoption.
Versioned report and adoption identities preserve exact whole-unit fingerprints
and the existing debt ratchet. Static source inspection never grants execution
authority or changes inspected source.

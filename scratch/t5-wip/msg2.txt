feat(raf-source): the impl blocks of every target

`Workspace::impls` lists every `impl` block of every target, inherent and of a
trait alike, once for each module path its file is reached under, sorted by
file, start, module path and target. Each is an `ImplBlock` with its `ImplId`
(its place in that order, which `Workspace::impl_block` takes back), its file,
the line of the `impl` keyword, its span from its first attribute to its
closing brace, its module and target, its test role, its written self type and
trait, the key of its self type (the struct, enum or union its self type path
names from its module, through aliases, empty when it names nothing the index
can place) and its members in source order.

The records are kept from the attaching of the impls and each is charged to
the item-bytes budget at the line of its `impl` keyword.

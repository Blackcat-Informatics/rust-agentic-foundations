feat(raf-okf): the reason of a change item

A change item takes the optional key `reason` after `outcome`, a non-empty line
of text read into `ChangeItem::reason`. It is accepted beside any outcome or
none, so an author may write it first; whether an outcome needs one is a rule of
strict mode, not of the profile. A reason that is empty, holds only spaces or is
not text is `Rule::ItemReasonEmpty` at `items`, which the workplan reports as
`okf-item-reason-empty`, and the placeholder rule reads the reason as it reads
the claim. The fix of `okf-item-unknown-key` names the three keys of an item.

The architecture page of the profile states the key in its `items` rule, and the
OKF profile capability states and binds it.

fix(raf-workplan): distinct claim keys, no separator claims, and every paragraph end of the claim rules

A repeated claim key takes the first of `-2`, `-3` and on that no other claim of
its section has or takes, so `**Foo.**`, `**Foo.**` and `**Foo 2.**` give `foo`,
`foo-3` and `foo-2` in that order and `foo-2`, `foo` and `foo-3` in the other,
and the key stays the identity of one claim.

A thematic break such as `* * *` or `---` and a list item with nothing after its
marker state nothing, so in the Constraints each ends the open unit and opens no
claim, as the content rule already reads them. The two rules now share one
reading of a list item and of a thematic break.

A comment line, such as a marker, ends a paragraph or a table of the
Constraints, so the paragraph after a marker is a claim of its own; a list item
goes on after one only with the lines indented to its content. A heading of any
level ends a paragraph of the Contract, so a bold lead directly after a `#####`
heading starts a clause.

The claims report and `coverage claims --capability` are pinned beside a second
delivered capability whose id sorts first: every claim of it comes first, and
`--capability` prints only the rows of the capability it names.
